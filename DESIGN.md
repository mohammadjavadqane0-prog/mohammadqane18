## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system crafts an upscale, tactile, and sensory e-commerce experience for a heritage artisanal purveyor of saffron, luxury nuts, dried delicacies, and handcrafted chocolates. It sits at the intersection of classical Persian culinary artistry and contemporary global minimalism. The experience reflects quiet wealth, authenticity, and gastronomic reverence. Every layout prioritizes breathing room, deliberate pacing, and physical tactility over aggressive promotional shouting.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
The target clientele comprises discerning epicures, premium gift buyers, and luxury corporate curators who value provenance, artisanal mastery, and bespoke packaging. The interface evokes warmth, olfactory anticipation, trustworthy heritage, and serene indulgence—akin to stepping into an opulent private boutique in an ancient bazaar reimagined through a clean architectural gallery lens.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Form
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism with Skeuomorphic Nuance:** Expansive ivory canvases (`#FAF6F0`) paired with deep botanical and royal undertones, punctuated by gilded accents.
- **Persian Architectural Geometry:** Subtle references to Persian fretwork (*Girih*) and fine illumination (*Tazhib*), distilled into minimal micro-borders, delicate hairline separators, and geometric framing rather than overwhelming ornamentation.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Glassine overlays, rich matte paper finishes, and ultra-diffused warm ambient shadows that honor the physical textures of embossed cardboard gift boxes, velvet ribbon wraps, and tin saffron caskets.
- **RTL-Native Ergonomics:** Balanced visual weight distribution calibrated specifically for Persian typography and right-to-left layout dynamics.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette is rooted in Persian culinary gold, fertile soil, and royal textiles:
- **Primary (`#7A1C28` — Deep Crimson / Anabi):** Derived from rich Persian pomegranates, dried barberries, and aged velvet. Used for primary interactive highlights, luxury badges, prominent CTAs, and pivotal focal moments.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` — Saffron Gold):** Embodying super-negin saffron threads and hand-applied gold leaf. Deployed for subtle embellishments, star ratings, premium tier highlights, and micro-framing borders.
- **Tertiary (`#3E5C43` — Pistachio Arbor):** A muted, deep pistachio green capturing freshly harvested Rafsanjan pistachio kernels and royal botanical gardens. Used for organic certification seals, freshness markers, and balanced secondary actions.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A1715` — Charred Charcoal / Black Walnut):** The primary text and high-contrast structural anchor, avoiding harsh absolute black in favor of deep roasted warmth.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#FAF6F0` — Royal Ivory):** Soft, warm-cast unbleached silk and cream paper tone serving as the global viewport background, mitigating ocular fatigue while elevating product colors.
- **Surface Elevation Containers:**
  - Base Surface: `#FAF6F0`
  - Container Low: `#F4EFE6`
  - Container Mid: `#EEE7DA`
  - Container High: `#FFFFFF` (for crisp product presentation cards)

### Color Application Rules
- Maintain an 80/15/5 ratio: 80% ivory surfaces and charcoal text, 15% crimson anchor structures, 5% golden luminescence.
- Never use pure black (`#000000`) for text or borders; preserve organic warmth throughout all typographic hierarchy.
- In dark interactive surfaces (such as night mode modal overlays or bespoke gift-box configurators), invert the background to `#161312` with text shifting to `#F4EFE6` and saffron gold (`#C59B27`) taking prominent focus.

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
The typographic ecosystem bridges literary heritage and crisp transaction clarity. For web implementations rendering Persian (Farsi), pairing system fonts with bespoke Persian weights (such as Shabnam, Dana, or IranNastaliq for ceremonial headlines) should adhere strictly to this metric scaling:

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers stately, bookish poise suited for artisanal product storytelling, harvest origins, and tasting profiles.
- **Longform Body Copy (`Vollkorn`):** Features wide apertures, generous ascenders, and warm counterspaces that mirror the organic curves of classical calligraphy while preserving high readability on screen.
- **Labels, Numerical Values & Functional Metadata (`Manrope`):** Clean, geometric neo-grotesque precision applied to prices, SKU codes, nutritional tables, grams/mithqals weight metrics, and cart calculations.

### RTL & Localization Specifications
- All Persian numerals (`۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹`) within prices and weights must be rendered with tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain absolute column alignment.
- Persian letter-spacing must remain strictly at `0` for connected cursive strings; do not apply positive kerning tracking to Nastaliq or Naskh typographic chains.
- Line heights across Farsi body text must be expanded by 10-15% relative to Latin norms to account for diacritics (*Erab*) and deep descenders (*Raa, Noon*).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The viewport utilizes a hybrid fluid-fixed 12-column grid system bounded by a maximum container width of `1380px` to maintain an intimate editorial gallery atmosphere on ultra-wide screens.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `3rem` (`48px`) margins. Generous vertical section padding (`4.5rem` to `6rem`) frames high-resolution product photography.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Collapses sidebar filters into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer margins. Product carousels transform into partial peek-over snap ribbons.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing operates on a strict 8-point base module, with 4px intervals allocated for compact metadata chips. The spatial atmosphere should mimic open courtyard architecture (*Sahn*): deliberate negative space separates disparate product categories, allowing saffron gift boxes, pistachio clusters, and artisanal chocolate bars to stand uncrowded like bespoke jewelry pieces.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Depth Strategy
Depth avoids synthetic drop shadows, leaning instead toward physical, diffused ambient light that simulates an artisan showroom:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAF6F0` (Royal Ivory canvas). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Product Foundation):** Used for resting product tiles and category selectors.
  - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(122, 28, 40, 0.02)`
  - Border: 1px hairline border in `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.18)` (subtle gold dust contour).
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Selection):** Used for focused cards, interactive luxury gift sets, and floating action headers.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.06)`
  - Transition: `box-shadow 300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), transform 300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`
  - Micro-lift: `-2px` translateY.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Sensory Tasting Drawer):** Used for quick-view overlays, weight selection sheets, and gift wrapping configurators.
  - Shadow: `0 24px 56px -8px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.16), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(122, 28, 40, 0.05)`
  - Backdrop: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(250, 246, 240, 0.82)`.

### Border Accents
Elements use fine hairlines (`1px`) styled with metallic warm gradients or muted brass tones (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.25)`) to echo gilded manuscript bindings and inlay woodwork (*Khatam*).

## Shapes

### Form Language
The visual identity implements a tailored **Soft (`1`)** shape metric. Sharp geometric precision reflects Persian tile work, while subtle soft radiuses (`0.25rem` / `4px` default) take the harsh industrial edge off interactive touchpoints.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Maintains an authoritative, formal luxury character.
- **Containers & Product Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Softens expansive surfaces while preserving crisp photographic framing.
- **Dialogs & Flyout Panels (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`). Ensures layered elegance on modern viewports.
- **Ornamental Exceptions:** Circular silhouettes (`50%`) are strictly reserved for weight increment dials (Mithqal counters), artisanal stamp seals, and botanical badge emblems.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury CTA (Add to Gift Box / Checkout):** Deep Crimson (`#7A1C28`) background, unbleached silk text (`#FAF6F0`), 1px subtle gold hairline inset border. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: shifts to `#621620` with a soft saffron glow.
- **Secondary (Tasting Notes / Explore Harvest):** Transparent background, Charcoal text (`#1A1715`), 1px solid brass border (`#C59B27`). Hover: fills with `#F4EFE6`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Charcoal text with a crimson underline positioned 4px below the text baseline. Hover: color transitions to Crimson (`#7A1C28`).

### Product Cards
- **Architecture:** White surface card (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm ivory surrounding canvas. 1px hairline border in `rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.15)`.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 vertical proportion showcasing custom packaging and raw botanicals.
- **Header Badges:** Positioned top-right (RTL), displaying grade marks such as "سوپر نگین قائنات" (Super Negin Saffron) in Saffron Gold (`#C59B27`) pill chips with crimson text.
- **Hover State:** Smooth lift `-3px`, shadow transition to Level 2, with image transitioning into a secondary sensory view (e.g., unopened luxury box cross-fading to open chocolate ganache texture).

### Chips & Badges
- **Taste Profile & Origin Chips:** Pistachio green tint background (`rgba(62, 92, 67, 0.08)`), dark green text (`#3E5C43`), `4px` border radius, padding `4px 10px`.
- **Harvest Vintage Badges:** Saffron gold background (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.12)`), deep mustard text (`#8A6B15`), featuring an integrated subtle flower motif icon.

### Inputs & Weight Steppers
- **Form Fields:** Pale ivory background (`#F4EFE6`), subtle bottom border in `#C59B27` (0.5 opacity) or full hairline box border with charcoal labels floating inside. Focused state transforms border to Deep Crimson (`#7A1C28`).
- **Persian Weight Selector (مثقال / گرم / کیلوگرم):** Segmented pill bar crafted in solid wood tones and ivory, providing tactile feedback when toggling between traditional Mithqal units and grams.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with `2px` corners for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Inactive: 1px border in `#1A1715` (0.3 opacity).
- Active: Filled with Deep Crimson (`#7A1C28`) with a secondary gold check indicator or inner dot.

### Specialized Artisanal Components
- **Gift Box Ribbon Configurator:** An interactive module enabling customers to select silk ribbon colors (Crimson, Emerald, Gold) and custom Persian calligraphic seal stamps (*Mohr*).
- **Sensory Tasting Scale:** A five-axis botanical radar visualization (Crunch, Bitterness, Aroma, Saltiness, Saffron Intensity) styled with thin gold lines.
